## Major Observations (WHAT Phase)

1. **Target size dominates recovery time**
   - 15bp recovers in 25-533 bars (median)
   - 200bp recovers in 265-542 bars (median)
   - Ratio: ~10x at short horizons, converges at long horizons

2. **5*H is NOT universal recovery window**
   - Works well for small targets (15bp: 79% at H=60)
   - Fails for large targets (200bp: 52% at H=60)
   - Need different multipliers for different targets

3. **MAE grows with waiting time**
   - At H=3: 65% have MAE < 30bp (small drawdown)
   - At H=480: 8% have MAE < 30bp, 50% have MAE > 100bp
   - Long waits correlate with large drawdowns

4. **Horizon matters less for large targets**
   - 200bp: Recovery patterns similar across H=3 to H=60
   - Most 200bp Case 3 need 250-500 bars regardless of H

5. **Recovery is binary by H=120**
   - At H=120: 100% of Case 3 recover within 5*H
   - At H=240: 100% within 2*H
   - Beyond H=120, all Case 3 are actually Case 2 or Case 1
