EXPERIMENT EXP-003: RSI Failure Deep Dive
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Find the ACTUAL cause of RSI overbought failures (11 failures in OOS 2024-2025).
Previous experiment (EXP-002) showed MA trend filter doesn't help.


WHAT WE TESTED
--------------
Analyzed all 11 failure cases to find common patterns:
- Volume at signal
- Momentum before signal
- RSI intensity
- Time of day / day of week
- Distance from moving averages


KEY FINDING: FAILURES HAVE WEAKER SIGNALS
==========================================

| Metric            | Failures | Successes | Difference |
|-------------------|----------|-----------|------------|
| Volume Ratio      | 1.22x    | 1.94x     | -37% LOWER |
| 5-bar Momentum    | 60 bps   | 82 bps    | -27% LOWER |
| RSI Value         | 84.0     | 83.2      | Same       |
| Dist from SMA20   | 0.97%    | 1.12%     | Same       |

INSIGHT: Failures happen when RSI overbought is triggered by WEAK moves.
- Low volume = weak conviction
- Low momentum = price drifting, not surging

Strong signals (high volume + high momentum) = RSI overbought works!
Weak signals (low volume + low momentum) = RSI overbought fails more


FILTER TEST RESULTS
===================

Tested various filters on OOS (2024-2025):

| Filter                         | Signals | Failures | Success% | Good Lost per Failure Avoided |
|--------------------------------|---------|----------|----------|-------------------------------|
| No Filter (Baseline)           | 664     | 11       | 98.3%    | -                             |
| Volume >= 0.5x                 | 601     | 7        | 98.8%    | 14.8                          |
| Volume >= 1.0x                 | 416     | 5        | 98.8%    | 40.3                          |
| Momentum >= 50 bps             | 382     | 4        | 99.0%    | 39.3                          |
| Vol >= 1.0x AND Mom >= 50 bps  | 304     | 2        | 99.3%    | 39.0                          |


THE TRADE-OFF PROBLEM
=====================

To avoid failures, we must sacrifice good signals:

1. Best ratio: Volume >= 0.5x
   - Avoids 4 failures (36%)
   - Loses 59 good signals
   - Ratio: 14.8 good signals lost per failure avoided

2. Most aggressive: Vol >= 1.0x AND Mom >= 50 bps
   - Avoids 9 failures (82%)
   - Loses 351 good signals (54%)
   - Keeps only 304 of 664 signals

3. No filter achieves ZERO failures


CONCLUSION
==========

FINDING VALIDATED: Weak signals (low volume/momentum) fail more often.

BUT the filter is NOT worth it:
- RSI already has 98.3% success rate
- Losing 15-40 good signals per failure avoided is a bad trade
- The 11 failures represent 1.7% of signals - very rare

RECOMMENDATION: Accept the 1.7% failure rate
- The cost of filtering is too high
- Focus on EXIT strategy to limit damage from rare failures
- Use stop loss / time exit to cap losses at ~50-100 bps


WHAT WE LEARNED
===============

1. RSI failures are caused by WEAK signals, not strong breakouts

2. Trend direction (EXP-002) is NOT the cause - correlation, not causation

3. Volume and momentum filters CAN reduce failures but at high cost

4. RSI overbought is already 98.3% reliable - hard to improve meaningfully

5. Better to accept rare failures + manage risk than over-filter


NEXT STEPS
==========

Since we can't easily PREVENT failures, focus on LIMITING DAMAGE:

Option A: Build complete RSI strategy with exit rules
- Entry: RSI overbought cross
- Exit: Target + Stop + Time limit
- Accept 1-2% failure rate, cap losses

Option B: Test RSI oversold failures (same analysis)
- Do oversold failures have similar patterns?
- Compare long vs short failure characteristics
