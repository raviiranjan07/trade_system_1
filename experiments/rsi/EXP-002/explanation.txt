EXPERIMENT EXP-002: RSI + MA Trend Filter
================================================================================

WHAT ARE WE TESTING?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We're testing if adding a Moving Average (MA) filter improves RSI signal quality.

The idea: RSI tells us when price is "oversold" (might bounce up) or "overbought"
(might drop). But maybe RSI fails when we're trading AGAINST the main trend.

So we test: What if we ONLY take RSI signals that align with the trend?
- Oversold (buy signal) → Only take if price is ABOVE the MA (uptrend)
- Overbought (sell signal) → Only take if price is BELOW the MA (downtrend)


WHY ARE WE TESTING THIS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From EXP-001 we found RSI has ~99% success rate. But we still have 1% failures.

Hypothesis: Those failures might be when we're trading against the main trend.
Example: RSI says "oversold, buy!" but we're in a strong downtrend that keeps
falling. The MA filter should catch this and skip those trades.


HOW DOES IT WORK?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Entry Rules:
   - RSI Alone (baseline): RSI(14) < 20 → GO LONG
   - RSI + MA Filter: RSI(14) < 20 AND price > SMA(period) → GO LONG

2. MA Periods Tested: SMA(20), SMA(50), SMA(100)

3. Horizon: 10 bars (150 minutes on 15min timeframe)


WHAT HAPPENED?
================================================================================

RESULT SUMMARY (Out-of-Sample 2024-2025):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Filter           | Signals | Bounce% | Failures | Avg MFE | Avg MAE |
|-----------------|---------|---------|----------|---------|---------|
| RSI Alone        |   596   |  99.7%  |    2     |  72.8   |  73.9   |
| RSI + SMA(20)    |    0    |   N/A   |   N/A    |   N/A   |   N/A   |
| RSI + SMA(50)    |   21    | 100.0%  |    0     |  53.7   |  60.5   |
| RSI + SMA(100)   |   63    | 100.0%  |    0     |  52.3   |  58.1   |


KEY FINDINGS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SMA(20) FILTER IS TOO STRICT
   - Zero signals! When RSI hits oversold (<20), price has dropped so much
     it's ALWAYS below SMA(20). This makes the filter useless.

2. SMA(50) AND SMA(100) FILTERS WORK... BUT ARE TOO AGGRESSIVE
   - SMA(50): Filters out 97% of signals (596 → 21)
   - SMA(100): Filters out 89% of signals (596 → 63)
   - Yes they achieve 100% bounce rate... but at what cost?

3. THE SURPRISE: RSI AGAINST TREND STILL WORKS!

   Out-of-Sample data:
   - RSI + SMA(50) AGAINST trend: 575 signals, 99.7% bounce rate
   - RSI + SMA(50) WITH trend: 21 signals, 100% bounce rate

   The difference is only 0.3%! RSI works well in BOTH directions.

4. MFE IS ACTUALLY LOWER WITH TREND
   - RSI Alone: Avg MFE = 72.8 bps
   - RSI + SMA(50) WITH trend: Avg MFE = 53.7 bps

   Why? When RSI is oversold but price > SMA, you're in a mild pullback.
   When RSI is oversold and price < SMA, you're in a deeper pullback with
   more room to bounce back (bigger MFE).


CONCLUSION:
================================================================================

HYPOTHESIS REJECTED!

The MA trend filter does NOT meaningfully improve RSI signal quality.

Before testing:
- We thought: "RSI fails against trend, filter will fix it"

After testing:
- Reality: RSI works ~99.7% regardless of trend direction
- MA filter removes 90-97% of opportunities
- Only improves success rate by 0.3% (99.7% → 100%)
- Actually REDUCES MFE (smaller bounces when WITH trend)

THE MATH:
- RSI Alone: 596 signals × 99.7% success = 594 good trades
- RSI + SMA(50): 21 signals × 100% success = 21 good trades
- We "saved" 2 failures but LOST 573 good trades!


WHAT DID WE LEARN?
================================================================================

1. RSI is robust to trend direction - works well both WITH and AGAINST trend

2. Over-filtering is dangerous - removing 97% of signals for 0.3% improvement
   is a bad trade-off

3. AGAINST trend signals have LARGER MFE - bigger bounces because of deeper
   mean reversion

4. The 1% RSI failures are NOT explained by trend direction - need to look
   for other causes (volume? volatility? specific price patterns?)

5. Simple is often better - RSI alone outperforms RSI + MA in opportunity count


WHAT SHOULD WE DO NEXT?
================================================================================

Option A: Investigate the RSI failures differently
   - Look at volume patterns during failures
   - Check if failures cluster in specific time periods
   - Analyze price structure before failure (trend strength, not just MA)

Option B: Test different MA-based entry signals
   - Instead of using MA as FILTER, use MA crossover as ENTRY
   - Combine with RSI for confirmation

Option C: Accept RSI as-is and focus on EXIT rules
   - RSI entry is 99.7% reliable
   - The bigger question is: how do we EXIT profitably?
   - This is where the real edge might be
