PART 1: TRAILING STOP TEST
================================================================================

What is a Trailing Stop?
------------------------
A trailing stop follows price as it moves in your favor, locking in profit.
Example: 15 bps trailing stop means exit when price drops 15 bps from peak.

LONG SIGNALS (BULL only, with 200 SMA filter):

| Trailing Stop | Avg Exit Profit | Avg Exit Bar | Max Possible | % Captured |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| No TS (Bar 10)|      +6.5 bps   |     10.0     |      N/A     |     N/A    |
| 10 bps        |     +15.5 bps   |      2.1     |    25.5 bps  |     61%    |
| 15 bps        |     +14.9 bps   |      2.9     |    29.7 bps  |     50%    |
| 20 bps        |     +18.2 bps   |      4.1     |    37.4 bps  |     49%    |
| 25 bps        |     +17.3 bps   |      5.1     |    40.1 bps  |     43%    |
| 30 bps        |     +16.7 bps   |      5.8     |    42.6 bps  |     39%    |

BEST FOR LONG: 20 bps trailing stop (+18.2 bps avg exit)

KEY FINDING:
------------
Trailing stop DRAMATICALLY improves average exit profit:
- LONG: +6.5 bps (no TS) -> +18.2 bps (TS=20) = +180% improvement
- SHORT: +9.6 bps (no TS) -> +23.2 bps (TS=30) = +142% improvement

Without trailing stop, profits are given back by Bar 10.
With trailing stop, we lock in gains before reversal.

PART 2: MFE vs MAE RELATIONSHIP
================================================================================

Question: Does early profit predict continuation or reversal?

LONG SIGNALS (126 trades, BULL only):

| Bar 1 Profit | Count | Bar 3 Avg | Bar 5 Avg | Bar 10 Avg | Pattern   |
|--------------|-------|-----------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| <0 bps       |     6 |    -6.0   |    -3.1   |     -1.6   | REVERSES  |
| 0-10 bps     |    53 |    +4.6   |    -1.1   |     -5.8   | REVERSES  |
| 10-20 bps    |    32 |   +20.0   |   +16.5   |     +8.4   | REVERSES  |
| 20-30 bps    |    15 |   +69.6   |   +82.4   |   +122.1   | CONTINUES |
| >30 bps      |    17 |   +62.0   |   +68.5   |    +58.9   | CONTINUES |

LONG INTERPRETATION:
- Small early profit (0-20 bps at Bar 1) -> Price REVERSES, ends lower
- Large early profit (>20 bps at Bar 1) -> Price CONTINUES, ends higher
- RULE: If Bar 1 shows >20 bps profit, HOLD. If <20 bps, use tight trailing stop.

SHORT SIGNALS (140 trades, BEAR only):

| Bar 1 Profit | Count | Bar 3 Avg | Bar 5 Avg | Bar 10 Avg | Pattern   |
|--------------|-------|-----------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| <0 bps       |     5 |    -1.6   |    +9.7   |    +22.7   | CONTINUES |
| 0-10 bps     |    52 |    +6.7   |   +10.3   |     +9.6   | FLAT      |
| 10-20 bps    |    38 |   +16.5   |   +21.2   |    +32.7   | CONTINUES |
| 20-30 bps    |    24 |   +25.1   |   +32.6   |    +49.4   | CONTINUES |
| >30 bps      |    19 |   +52.2   |   +42.7   |    +16.1   | REVERSES  |

SHORT INTERPRETATION:
- Most SHORT signals CONTINUE (profit grows over time)
- EXCEPT when Bar 1 shows >30 bps (too fast) -> REVERSES
- RULE: If Bar 1 shows >30 bps, EXIT. Otherwise, HOLD with trailing stop.


================================================================================
SUMMARY: EXIT STRATEGY RULES
================================================================================

LONG SIGNALS (RSI oversold cross, price > SMA200):
-------------------------------------------------
IF Bar 1 profit > 20 bps:
    -> HOLD: Price likely to continue higher
    -> Use 20-25 bps trailing stop to protect
ELSE:
    -> Use 10-15 bps trailing stop (price likely to reverse)

BEST OVERALL: 20 bps trailing stop = +18.2 bps avg exit


SHORT SIGNALS (RSI overbought cross, price < SMA200):
----------------------------------------------------
IF Bar 1 profit > 30 bps:
    -> EXIT: Price likely to reverse
ELSE:
    -> HOLD: Price likely to continue
    -> Use 30 bps trailing stop to protect

BEST OVERALL: 30 bps trailing stop = +23.2 bps avg exit
