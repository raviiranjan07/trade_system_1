2) WHY THESE FILTERS? (from investigate_long_vs_short.py)
==================================================

V1 LONG loses in two conditions:
  - Choppy markets: EMA sep < 0.5% → 30 trades, 43% win, -268 bps
  - Quiet markets: Small bars, low ATR → poor trade quality

V1.2 filters remove these losing conditions:
  - ATR percentile >= 25: Skip quiet/low-volatility markets
  - EMA50/200 separation >= 0.5%: Skip choppy/range-bound markets
