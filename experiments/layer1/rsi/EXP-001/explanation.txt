EXPERIMENT EXP-001: RSI Mean Reversion Behavior Analysis
================================================================================

WHAT ARE WE TESTING?
--------------------
We're testing if RSI (Relative Strength Index) can predict when price will
bounce or drop. RSI is a number from 0 to 100 that measures how "stretched"
the price is:

- RSI below 20-30 = "Oversold" = Price dropped too fast, might bounce UP
- RSI above 70-80 = "Overbought" = Price went up too fast, might drop DOWN

Think of it like a rubber band - when stretched too far, it snaps back.

We tested 7 different RSI settings:
1. RSI(7) with 30/70 thresholds (fast, loose)
2. RSI(7) with 20/80 thresholds (fast, tight)
3. RSI(14) with 30/70 thresholds (standard, loose)
4. RSI(14) with 20/80 thresholds (standard, tight)
5. RSI(14) with 40/60 thresholds (standard, very loose)
6. RSI(21) with 30/70 thresholds (slow, loose)
7. RSI(21) with 20/80 thresholds (slow, tight)


WHY ARE WE TESTING THIS?
------------------------
Mean reversion is one of the simplest trading ideas: prices don't go in one
direction forever. When price moves too fast in one direction, it often
reverses (at least temporarily).

RSI captures this by measuring momentum. When RSI hits extreme levels:
- Buyers/sellers are exhausted
- The move is "overdone"
- A reversal is likely

This is ATOMIC testing - we're only testing RSI by itself, no other
indicators or filters. We want to know: does RSI alone have predictive power?


HOW DOES IT WORK?
-----------------
1. Signal Detection:
   - When RSI crosses BELOW threshold (20 or 30) = Oversold signal
   - When RSI crosses ABOVE threshold (70 or 80) = Overbought signal

2. What We Measured:
   - "Bounce Rate" = After oversold, did price go UP within next 10 bars?
   - "Drop Rate" = After overbought, did price go DOWN within next 10 bars?
   - "Average Move" = How much did price move in the expected direction?

3. Time Horizons:
   - H=3 (45 minutes), H=5 (1.25 hours), H=10 (2.5 hours),
   - H=15 (3.75 hours), H=20 (5 hours)

4. Data Periods:
   - In-Sample (training): 2020-2023 (140,010 candles)
   - Out-of-Sample (testing): 2024-2025 (69,869 candles)


WHAT HAPPENED?
--------------
** IN-SAMPLE RESULTS (2020-2023) at H=10: **

| RSI Config    | Signals | Bounce Rate | Avg Move |
|---------------|---------|-------------|----------|
| RSI(7) 30/70  | 9,028   | 99.3%       | 74 bps   |
| RSI(7) 20/80  | 4,975   | 99.3%       | 80 bps   |
| RSI(14) 30/70 | 3,836   | 99.5%       | 82 bps   |
| RSI(14) 20/80 | 1,121   | 99.3%       | 99 bps   |
| RSI(14) 40/60 | 7,742   | 99.1%       | 73 bps   |
| RSI(21) 30/70 | 1,926   | 99.3%       | 93 bps   |
| RSI(21) 20/80 | 385     | 99.5%       | 110 bps  |

** OUT-OF-SAMPLE RESULTS (2024-2025) at H=10: **

| RSI Config    | Signals | Bounce Rate | Avg Move |
|---------------|---------|-------------|----------|
| RSI(7) 30/70  | 4,394   | 98.9%       | 57 bps   |
| RSI(7) 20/80  | 2,471   | 98.8%       | 57 bps   |
| RSI(14) 30/70 | 1,902   | 98.7%       | 60 bps   |
| RSI(14) 20/80 | 596     | 99.7%       | 73 bps   |
| RSI(14) 40/60 | 3,769   | 98.8%       | 54 bps   |
| RSI(21) 30/70 | 1,043   | 98.7%       | 62 bps   |
| RSI(21) 20/80 | 176     | 98.9%       | 77 bps   |


WHAT DID WE LEARN?
------------------
1. RSI MEAN REVERSION WORKS CONSISTENTLY
   - Bounce/drop rates are 98-99% across ALL combinations
   - This held true in BOTH in-sample AND out-of-sample periods
   - Not luck - it's a real pattern in BTC 15-minute data

2. TIGHTER THRESHOLDS = BIGGER MOVES (but fewer signals)
   - RSI(21) 20/80: Only 385 signals, but 110 bps average move
   - RSI(14) 40/60: 7,742 signals, but only 73 bps average move
   - Trade-off: Quality vs Quantity

3. ALL MOVES ARE ABOVE MINIMUM PROFITABLE (12 bps)
   - Smallest average move: 54 bps (RSI(14) 40/60 out-of-sample)
   - Largest average move: 110 bps (RSI(21) 20/80 in-sample)
   - After 8 bps fees, still profitable on paper

4. OUT-OF-SAMPLE MOVES ARE SMALLER BUT STILL SIGNIFICANT
   - In-sample average: 73-110 bps
   - Out-of-sample average: 54-77 bps
   - ~30% reduction, but still well above 12 bps minimum

5. BEST BALANCE: RSI(14) 20/80
   - Decent signal count: ~600-1,100 signals
   - High bounce rate: 99.3-99.7%
   - Good move size: 73-99 bps
   - Standard RSI period (widely used)


IMPORTANT CAVEATS
-----------------
This is BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS, not a trading strategy yet!

We measured: "Does price move in expected direction after RSI signal?"
We did NOT measure: "Can we actually capture that profit?"


================================================================================
EDGE CASE ANALYSIS (Deep Dive)
================================================================================

We analyzed RSI(14) 20/80 in detail to understand the risks.

EDGE CASE 1: FAILURE CASES (When RSI Signal Doesn't Work)
---------------------------------------------------------
Failures are RARE but EXPENSIVE when they happen:

| Signal     | Period         | Failures | Avg Loss | Max Loss | Pattern           |
|------------|----------------|----------|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Oversold   | In-Sample      | 8 (0.7%) | 208 bps  | 476 bps  | 87% in DOWNTREND  |
| Oversold   | Out-of-Sample  | 2 (0.3%) | 134 bps  | 160 bps  | 100% in DOWNTREND |
| Overbought | In-Sample      | 16 (1.2%)| 256 bps  | 697 bps  | 100% in UPTREND   |
| Overbought | Out-of-Sample  | 11 (1.7%)| 142 bps  | 468 bps  | 100% in UPTREND   |

KEY INSIGHT: ALL failures happen when trading AGAINST strong trend!
- Oversold failures = trying to go LONG in a strong DOWNTREND
- Overbought failures = trying to go SHORT in a strong UPTREND


EDGE CASE 2: DRAWDOWN BEFORE BOUNCE (MAE) - CRITICAL!
-----------------------------------------------------
Even for WINNING trades that eventually bounced, price often drops first.
MAE = Maximum Adverse Excursion = worst drawdown during the trade

| Metric              | In-Sample | Out-of-Sample |
|---------------------|-----------|---------------|
| Mean drawdown       | 121 bps   | 74 bps        |
| Median drawdown     | 65 bps    | 46 bps        |
| 90th percentile     | 270 bps   | 169 bps       |
| 95th percentile     | 382 bps   | 232 bps       |

STOP LOSS IMPACT (% of WINNING trades that would be stopped out):
| Stop Loss Level | In-Sample | Out-of-Sample |
|-----------------|-----------|---------------|
| -10 bps         | 91%       | 85%           |
| -15 bps         | 86%       | 80%           |
| -20 bps         | 81%       | 75%           |
| -25 bps         | 78%       | 70%           |
| -50 bps         | 59%       | 47%           |

KEY INSIGHT: A tight stop loss will KILL most trades!
- At -15bp stop: 80-86% of winning trades stopped out
- At -50bp stop: still 47-59% stopped out
- Need WIDE stop (or no stop) to let bounce develop


EDGE CASE 3: TIME TO REVERSAL
-----------------------------
How long does it take for the bounce to happen?

| Metric                | In-Sample   | Out-of-Sample |
|-----------------------|-------------|---------------|
| Mean time to best     | 5.2 bars    | 5.5 bars      |
| (in minutes)          | (77 min)    | (83 min)      |
| Within 3 bars         | 40%         | 36%           |
| Within 5 bars         | 54%         | 50%           |
| Within 7 bars         | 69%         | 64%           |

KEY INSIGHT: You need to WAIT. Half of bounces take 5+ bars (1+ hour).


EDGE CASE 4: OUTCOME DISTRIBUTION (What targets are realistic?)
---------------------------------------------------------------
What % of signals reach different profit targets?

| Target    | In-Sample | Out-of-Sample |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| +12 bps   | 91%       | 88%           |
| +15 bps   | 89%       | 86%           |
| +25 bps   | 80%       | 74%           |
| +50 bps   | 59%       | 50%           |
| +100 bps  | 31%       | 23%           |

KEY INSIGHT: Target 25 bps is realistic (74-80% reach it).
Target 50 bps is possible but only 50-59% reach it.


EDGE CASE 5: TREND IMPACT
-------------------------
Most oversold signals happen in DOWNTRENDs (counter-trend trading).

| Trading Direction     | Signals | Bounce Rate | Avg MFE | Avg MAE |
|-----------------------|---------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Long in UPTREND       | 22      | 96%         | 76 bps  | 52 bps  |
| Long in DOWNTREND     | 1,099   | 99%         | 100 bps | 123 bps |

KEY INSIGHT: Counter-trend (long in downtrend) actually has HIGHER MFE
but also HIGHER drawdown. The bounce is bigger but you suffer more first.


IMPLICATIONS FOR EXIT RULES
---------------------------
Based on edge case analysis:

1. TIGHT STOP LOSS WON'T WORK
   - Stops out 70-86% of winners
   - Need wide stop (50+ bps) or no stop at all

2. TIME-BASED EXIT MIGHT BE BETTER
   - Wait 5-10 bars for bounce to develop
   - Don't exit too early

3. REALISTIC TARGETS
   - 25 bps target: 74-80% success
   - 50 bps target: 50-59% success

4. TREND FILTER COULD HELP
   - Skip signals against strong trend to avoid rare but big losses
   - But this also removes some of the biggest bounces
